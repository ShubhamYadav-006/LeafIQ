# Leaf AI — Development Rules

**Project:** Leaf AI  
**Product:** Evidence-Based Crop Health Assessment  
**Stack:** PERN  
**Status:** Hackathon MVP

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

This document defines the rules that must be followed while developing Leaf AI.

These rules exist to ensure that:

- The product remains focused.
- The AI behaves responsibly.
- The implementation remains technically credible.
- The UI remains simple.
- The team does not introduce unnecessary features.
- The live demo remains reliable.
- Frontend, backend, database, and AI layers remain consistent.

These rules are mandatory unless explicitly changed by the project team.

Leaf AI

## 2.2 Product Positioning

Leaf AI is:

> An AI-powered crop health assessment platform that combines leaf-image analysis with farmer observations to provide an explainable, uncertainty-aware assessment and practical next steps.
